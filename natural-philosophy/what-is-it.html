The ideas behind the conversation

What is it?

This material is preserved from the archive. Details, including event information, may have changed; check the linked venues for current information.

Pub/Café Philosophy provides an open, informal space where anyone—from seasoned thinkers to curious newcomers—can gather over drinks to explore life's biggest questions.

Rooted in the living tradition of natural philosophy, our approach treats philosophical inquiry not as dry academic dogma, but as an active, organic exploration of ourselves, our world, and how we make sense of reality. Rather than listening to lectures, participants engage directly in rational inquiry and collaborative debate.

Our Core Principles

  • Democratic Topic Selection: The question of the evening is proposed and chosen democratically by the group at the start of each session.
  • Equal, Open Participation: You do not need formal philosophical training to take part. The sessions are designed to make inquiry fun, accessible, and meaningful for everyone.
  • Philosophy for Philosophy's Sake: The aim is not necessarily to reach a final resolution or absolute proof, but to enjoy the stimulation of dancing with ideas, challenging unexamined assumptions, and broadening perspectives.
  • Facilitation Over Lecturing: Sessions are guided by a facilitator whose role is simply to pose questions, encourage balanced discussion, and ensure everyone has a voice without defending any dogmatic position.
  • Mutual Respect: Participants are asked to express their ideas clearly and allow others to speak without interruption or hostility.
