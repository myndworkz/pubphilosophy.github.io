# Natural Philosophy archive

The homepage is the interactive Natural Philosophy map, adapted from
`myndworkz/my-website/natural-philosophy.html` and its `style.css`. Open the
golden sphere to reveal the archive pages; click a floating philosopher card
for details. Use the bubble-speed button to pause, slow, or resume the motion.

The text archive is at `library.html`. It preserves `what-is-it.html`,
`history-philosophy.html`, `events.html` and the archived
`Archive/static_site/philosophy_workshops.html` from the source repository.
Event information is historical and should be checked with the venues.

Open `index.html` in a browser to preview. Alternatively, serve the repository
root with `python -m http.server 8000` and visit
`http://localhost:8000/natural-philosophy/`.
